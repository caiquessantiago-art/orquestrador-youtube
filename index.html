Orquestrador The Fragile Human

A private, single-channel tool for the YouTube channel The Fragile Human.

What it is

This is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Fragile Human. It is not a public service: no one else can sign in to it or use it, and it does not access any other channel.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel (about one long video and three Shorts per week), filling in title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Reads the performance metrics of the owner's own channel (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate, audience retention) through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs.
  • Writes a weekly summary of those numbers for the owner, with suggestions on what to improve in the next videos.
